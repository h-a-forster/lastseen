# Evaluation plan (written 28 Oct 2026, amended 29 and 30 Oct; all before the final run)

This fixes, in advance, how the headline comparison is run and reported, so that the endpoints, scenarios and wording
can't be chosen after seeing the results. The final run happens once, after the feature freeze, and every endpoint
below is published whatever it shows. What changed on 29 Oct, and why, is listed at the end.

## Question
Does Muse Spark's reading of the witness reports help a search find a missing person sooner?

## What is compared
Two comparisons are fixed in advance. Each gets its own interval and its own wording.
1. **With and without Spark, same planner.** The planner is the team's planner, `hedge`: the expanding square
   until a witness sighting, then one drone hunts with the map planner `ours`. Without Spark it never hunts, so it
   flies exactly the spiral, and this comparison is also the team's planner against the spiral.
2. **With and without Spark, expanding square.** `spiral+spark` restarts the expanding square on the block Spark's
   newest reading makes most likely; `spiral` ignores the reports. This tests Spark's reading without depending on
   how good the planner is.

For context: the planner with Spark against `spiral+spark` (does keeping a map beat restarting a fixed pattern on the
same readings?), the map planner on its own with and without Spark (`ours`, `ours+spark`), and the fixed patterns
lawnmower, spiral, concentric and sector.

## Scenarios
- 200 held-out dementia scenarios, seeds **900030-900229**, 3 km map. None of these seeds has been run before.
  Seeds 900000-900029 were looked at during development and are not part of this evaluation.
- Witness reports are generated by `app/sar/cues.py` as frozen in commit `50851bf` (before any model read them).
- Imagery mode: every photo with the person in it is decided by a recorded SAM 3.1 + Muse Spark answer on a real drone
  photo of a person that size (`app/perception/pool.py`); the detection table is `data/pod_table.json` as committed at
  the freeze.
- **One drone** (the primary setting), and three drones (secondary), at 100 m, 25 min per battery, 3 min turnaround
  at base, 4 hours of search.
- Spark readings are fetched live once under a $4 cap, then replayed from the cache for every planner. Readings that
  fail are counted and reported; those runs carry on without hypotheses.

## Endpoints
**Primary**, for each of the two comparisons, with **one drone**: share of scenarios where the person is found within
**2 hours**, with Spark minus without Spark, paired by scenario, with a 95% paired-bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples, generator seed
0). Two hours because survival falls with time and because that is where a reading can still change where the drones
go.

**Secondary** (reported, not used for the headline):
- found within 1 hour and within 4 hours, same comparisons;
- **three drones:** both comparisons at 1, 2 and 4 hours, with three drones instead of one (the second command below);
- time-to-find curves (Kaplan-Meier) and median time to find;
- every planner against the spiral at 1, 2 and 4 hours;
- per search: photos taken, alarms raised for a reviewer, photos with the person in them that raised no alarm;
- per person found: drone flight hours, photos and alarms; and the share of drone time spent flying;
- the primary endpoint split by whether the sighting report was correct, wrong, or absent;
- **a lost drone:** with three drones, found within 2 hours when one drone fails at a seeded time in the first 2
  hours (the third command below).

## How results will be worded
- **"Helps"** only if the primary interval excludes zero.
- **"Promising"** if the estimate is above zero but the interval includes zero.
- **"No evidence of a gain"** otherwise, and said as plainly as a gain would be.
- The share of scenarios where the person had already left the mapped area when the search started (the ceiling) is
  printed with every table.

## Commands
```
uv run python -m app.sar.bench --set heldout --offset 30 --seeds 200 --category dementia --imagery --workers 8 \
  --planners lawnmower,spiral,concentric,sector,spiral+spark,ours,ours+spark,hedge,hedge+spark \
  --reference spiral --drones 1 --usd-cap 4 --json docs/results/final.json
uv run python -m app.sar.bench --set heldout --offset 30 --seeds 200 --category dementia --imagery --workers 8 \
  --planners lawnmower,spiral,concentric,sector,spiral+spark,ours,ours+spark,hedge,hedge+spark \
  --reference spiral --drones 3 --usd-cap 4 --json docs/results/final-three-drones.json
uv run python -m app.sar.bench --set heldout --offset 30 --seeds 200 --category dementia --imagery --workers 8 \
  --planners spiral,spiral+spark,hedge,hedge+spark --reference spiral --drones 3 --lose-drone --usd-cap 4 \
  --json docs/results/final-lost-drone.json
```
The bench prints both primary comparisons itself (every `<name>+spark` run next to `<name>`), with the split by
sighting report. Results are committed to `docs/results/` with the commit they were produced from. The JSON holds every scenario's
find times and which report details were true, so every number above can be recomputed from it.

## Known limits
- The scenarios are synthetic maps with a simulated walker; detection rates come from one dataset of staged scenes.
- Forest detection is an assumption (0.4 of open ground), not a measurement.
- Repeated photos of the same spot on different sorties are treated as independent chances.
- A leg can clip the corner of a no-fly cell by under 1.25 m (the route check samples every 1/20 of a cell).

## Changes on 29 Oct (before any held-out seed was run)
- **Height 60 m → 100 m.** The altitude sweep on development seeds (`docs/parameters.md`) found more people within
  2 hours at 100-120 m than at 60 m for every planner; 100 m leaves a margin under the usual 120 m legal ceiling.
- **Comparison 2 added**, with `spiral+spark` also set against the planner with Spark.
- **Added secondary endpoints:** cost per person found, and a lost drone.
- **Planners may now re-plan drones in the air** when a report comes in (`retask` in `app/sar/interface.py`). Both
  `spiral+spark` and the probability-map planner use it; planners that ignore the reports are unchanged.
- **Detection re-measured with more photos:** every test-split tile at 2x shrink, so the size flown at 100 m rests
  on 227 people instead of 50 (79%, was 78%). The development runs in `docs/parameters.md` used the earlier table.
- **Spark's evidence may quote the map description it is shown** ("streams and ponds"), not only the reports. Before,
  such hypotheses were dropped, and on some scenarios the whole reading of the brief was lost. Plain planners are
  unchanged; development numbers with Spark were re-run.
- A bug that could stop fixed patterns flying for the rest of a search was fixed (commit `c912676`); all
  development numbers were re-run after it.

## Changes on 30 Oct (before any held-out seed was run)
- **The team's planner is now `hedge`.** On 200 development seeds the map planner `ours` with Spark matched the
  spiral at 2 hours but found fewer people in the first hour. `hedge` flies the spiral until a sighting, then sends
  one drone after Spark's reading with `ours`: with three drones, 71% found within 2 hours against the spiral's 68%,
  and no worse than the spiral whether the sighting was right, wrong or absent; with one drone, 53% against 48%.
- **The primary setting is now one drone; three drones become a secondary endpoint.** With three drones on a 3 km
  map, most people the spiral misses within 2 hours have already walked off the map, so the order of search changes
  little. One drone is the common case for a volunteer team, and the case where the order of search matters.
