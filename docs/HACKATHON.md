## Rules
- Build toward one problem statement:
  - **Build for Your World**: Meta's models in a domain you know firsthand.
  - **Push the Frontier**: software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials, physical systems.
- Must use Meta's models (Model API and/or open weights Muse Glimmer / Muse Spark Open).
- Public repo. Teams of up to 4.
- New work only; the demo must make clear what was built during the event, or we're disqualified.
- Anti-projects (don't build): medical advice of any kind, basic RAG, simple image analysers, simple education chatbots, job-application screeners, nutrition coaches, personality analysers, sports analysers or coaches, anything political or about war.

## Judging
1. Pass/fail: fits a problem statement and really uses Meta models.
2. Scored 0-10, **equally weighted**:
   - **Impact**: real-world potential; who benefits; could people actually use it?
   - **Use of Meta AI tooling**: creative, beyond a basic integration, surprising capabilities.
   - **Technical depth and execution**: sound, refined engineering.
   - **Demo**: works live; the video is genuinely cool to watch.
3. Top 5 per track: final judges watch the videos live and rank them.
