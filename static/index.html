From what witnesses say to where the drone searches, in seconds.

LastSeen flies a search drone for a missing person and decides where it looks. Today, what witnesses say reaches the search map by hand, or not at all. One volunteer launches it, swaps batteries and stays in command.

  • Muse Spark reads every reportEach witness's words become weighted, checked scenarios on the real map, in about 20 seconds.
  • The drone re-plans mid-flightIt breaks off its search pattern to follow a sighting, and flies the search itself.
  • SAM 3.1 and Muse Spark check every photoFor a person a few dozen pixels wide: a reviewer sees about 280 close-ups instead of 1,250 photos.

Tested in simulation on 200 searches fixed in advance, with detection measured on real drone photos. The search below runs over a real Swiss valley.

            Try it on any place

            Run a test search of your own. Click anywhere on the map and LastSeen builds a 3 km search area around that point. A simulated test subject walks off from it the way you choose, and both planners race to find her with one drone. A witness phones in when you choose, in the simulator's words or your own; watch Muse Spark read the report and the team planner's drone break off. The race plays in section 1.

            A new place takes about a minute: its map comes from OpenStreetMap's free servers, which can be busy; LastSeen then tries once more. Coasts and places with little map detail are refused, and aerial photos exist only for Switzerland.

            Where the witness saw her (optional)

            Muse Spark reads only these words, never your mark. Say it as a passer-by would: roughly how far and which way from where she left (shown above once you mark), and what was there, like a road, a river or a village, bridge or chapel named on the map.

            Live model calls are off on this server, so this needs the hosted demo.

            How the test works

            • LastSeen writes the family's brief from her simulated walk: which way a neighbour saw her set off, and whether she walks a lot. The brief and the witness are both right.
            • With your mark, she walks there from the starting point, arriving as the witness sees her, 5 minutes before the call, then wanders on while she still has the strength.
            • Without a mark, LastSeen picks a walk that heads her your way and writes the witness's words too.
            • Under trees a drone photo often can't show her, as in a real search: mark her in a forest and she may stay hidden even when the drone flies over her.
            • Afterwards, tick "Reveal where the person really was" in section 1 to see her real path.
