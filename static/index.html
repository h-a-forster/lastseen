From what witnesses say to where the drone searches, in seconds.

LastSeen flies a search drone for a missing person and decides where it looks. Today, what witnesses say reaches the search map by hand, or not at all. LastSeen closes that gap: Muse Spark turns every report into weighted, checked scenarios in seconds, and the drone re-plans from them and flies the search itself, breaking off mid-flight to follow a sighting. SAM 3.1 and Muse Spark check every photo for a person a few dozen pixels wide, so a reviewer sees about 270 close-ups instead of 1,200 photos. One volunteer launches it, swaps batteries and stays in command. Tested end to end in simulation, over a real valley and real drone photos.

          Search a place you know

          Click the map below where someone was last seen. LastSeen turns the 3 km around it into a search area and races both planners there with one drone; the race plays in section 1.

          Takes about a minute. The map data comes from OpenStreetMap's free servers, which limit how often they can be asked: if they're busy, try again shortly. Coasts and places with little map detail are refused, and aerial photos exist only for Switzerland.
